fix: one silent fixture no longer starves its node port; flag node faults

Root cause (RDM-LOG36). Three Martin ERA 800s share one port of node
2.11.90.6. 4D50:00115938 is a faulty unit (owner confirmed with a second
controller): 123 requests, zero replies. Benny512 allows one in-flight
command per node port, each silent command held the port for 1.5 s x 3
attempts, and nothing stopped callers re-asking the dead fixture - from
15:22:33 to the end of the capture 100% of the port's traffic went to it
and the two healthy fixtures starved ("rows stall and spin"). Every reply
from the healthy fixtures was valid: starvation, not corruption.

Changes (owner decisions 2026-10-06: move a fixture that stops answering
to the end of the queue, mark it, recheck occasionally; shorten the
timeout; flag node-level faults):

- ProfileDirect response timeout 1500 ms -> 500 ms, retries stay 2.
  6,754 matched replies across LOG2-LOG36 (wired and CRMX): slowest
  first-attempt reply 96 ms; every later reply was a lost packet answered
  after a retransmit.
- The per-device breaker also opens on silence attributable to ONE device:
  a silent command counts only if another command on the same node port
  was answered since that device's previous strike or answer. Three
  strikes -> paused (CauseNoResponse), queued commands fail fast. A node
  reboot (RDM-LOG4) silences everyone, so no second strike can count;
  TestTimeoutsDoNotOpenTheBreaker passes unmodified.
  docs/decisions/0001-silence-pauses-a-device-only-with-link-evidence.md
- After a silent command the fixture's other pending commands move behind
  every other fixture's on the port.
- Automatic recheck: one GET DEVICE_INFO after the cool-down (15 s,
  doubling to 2 min while the port is proven alive), skipped while the ToD
  no longer lists the fixture, immediate when a ToD lists it again. On
  recovery the background reader re-reads its identity.
- Responses whose Destination UID is not ours are ignored and counted
  (E1.20 response addressing; section number not verified here). LOG36's
  second controller (2.11.52.172) produced one carrying the exact TN, source
  UID and command class of our earlier TN 9 GET DEVICE_INFO to ...38. Every
  response addressed to us in LOG2-LOG36 carries dst=7FF0:00000001.
- ArtRdm datagrams whose RDM message will not decode are recorded against
  the sending node (NodeFaults, MalformedRDM): LOG36's 2.11.90.6 sent four
  239-byte ArtRdm-framed datagrams carrying ArtPollReply text. Shown as a
  "Node fault - malformed RDM" pill and sentence on Nodes, one deduplicated
  NOTE in the RDM log, and in /api/diagnostics/rdm.
- Devices: no-response wording distinct from the CRMX proxy-refusal
  sentence (which is unchanged).
