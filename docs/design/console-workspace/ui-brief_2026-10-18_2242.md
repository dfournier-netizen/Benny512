## 1. Why

The owner bench-tested v0.2.1 on a real 90-fixture show. On a laptop (1440 × 900):

- **The grid gets little room.** It starts about 600 px down the page, and about 250 px of it is visible.
- **The page is about 5,400 px tall.** The Controls panel and the Tests panel stack under the grid, so the operator scrolls a long way.
- **Tiles don't identify fixtures.** They are labelled with the truncated manufacturer name ("Elat…"), not the FID and type.
- **Plan spacing wastes most of the screen.** Positions come from the MVR, so most of the grid is empty squares; the floor layer showed 1 of its 26 fixtures on screen.
- **Toggle labels add clutter.** Every toggle carries its state as text ("Lasso: OFF", "Add mode: OFF", "Ghosts: OFF").

Owner's request: "the selection grid taking up more screen real estate and our fixture control windows to become floating, hideable windows… increase functional visibility of the grid… Tests can move to its own screen… de-clutter the UI and make the console area easier to use."

## 2. Owner decisions (Dom, 2026-10-18 22:33)

1. **Laptop first.** Design for 1024–1920 px wide. Phone and tablet layouts come later; until then they keep today's behaviour.
2. **Mockup first.** The owner approves the mockup before any production change.
3. **Only Controls opens by default.** Selection order, Groups & presets and MIDI start hidden.
4. **Compact spacing.** There is a **Plan / Compact** switch. Plan keeps the MVR spacing. Compact packs fixtures tight and ignores the real spacing.
5. **Tests gets its own tab** in the main navigation, beside Console.

Already confirmed before this brief, and still binding:
- dark at Arm
- Master as the overall dimmer
- part faders under each type's Master
- the bottom group-fader bar on every screen

## 4. What to design

Each item says what it is, what the mockup does, and what you must specify. Keep to the house rules in §6.

### 4.1 Header, one row
- Brand, then show name and adapter, then the connection pill ("Connected · Shared control"). Below 1280 px the adapter text and "· Shared control" drop out.
- **Output cluster:** the state word (`DISARMED · no output` / `ARMED · LIVE` and the other states in component-specs §1), then the **ARM pill**, then the **DISARM BLACKOUT slab**. The three sit in one bordered inset group, always at the right, and never move or wrap.
- **Show ▾** menu holds Show tools… and Fixture library… (today these are two header buttons).
- Spec: the cluster at 1024 / 1280 / 1440 / 1920, and the order things drop when space runs out. The Disarm slab never drops.

### 4.2 Workspace toolbar
- **Layer tabs:** a segmented control with each layer's name and count (`Truss 6.4 m · 44`). Today this is a row of big chips.
- **Spacing:** a `Plan | Compact` segmented control (§4.5).
- **Zoom:** `Fit | − | +`. Fit is a toggle and is on by default. Pressing − or + turns Fit off.
- **Lasso** and **Add:** toggle buttons in the new ON style (§4.3). Today they read "Lasso: OFF" and "Add mode: OFF".
- **Select ▾:**
  - Select all fixtures
  - Select this layer
  - Select none
  - Invert
  - Pick whole fixtures / Pick cells (today's parent/cell switch)
- **View ▾:**
  - Ghosts (checkbox item)
  - Names on tiles (checkbox item)
  - Live values on tiles (checkbox item)
  - Edit layout…
  - Reload layout from MVR
- **Windows ▾:** a checkbox item per floating window, plus "Reset window positions". It sits at the far right.
- At 1024 px the mockup wraps to two rows. **Propose a better 1024 composition** (for example icon + short word, or merging View into Select). Keep the 44 px coarse-pointer target.

### 4.3 Toggle ON state (app-wide pattern for these buttons)
- The mockup shows ON with:
  - a check mark before the label
  - a 2 px accent border
  - bold label text
  - the pressed fill (`--b5-color-bg-pressed`)
- `aria-pressed` carries the state. This replaces the "X: OFF/ON" words.
- **Deliverable:** a `b5-icon-check` symbol (the mockup uses a "✓" character) and the spec. Check it in Dark, Daylight and greyscale: ON and OFF must be told apart without hue.

### 4.4 Floating window (new component)
Used for Controls, Selection order, Groups & presets and MIDI encoders. Later, possibly Raw DMX and Tools.

Mockup behaviour:
- **Chrome:**
  - title bar: drag-handle icon, title and a subtitle ("3 selected")
  - **Collapse:** folds to the title bar only
  - **Close / hide:** ✕
- **Moving:** drag by the title bar. With the title bar focused, arrow keys move the window 10 px and Shift+arrow moves it 40 px. The window stays inside the workspace.
- **Stacking:** clicking a window brings it to the front.
- **Memory:** open, collapsed and position are remembered per browser.
- **Default positions:**
  - Controls: right edge, 440 px wide
  - Selection order: top-left
  - Groups: below Selection order
  - MIDI: bottom-left
- **Max height:** the workspace minus 16 px. The body scrolls inside.
- **Hidden windows** come back from Windows ▾.

Specify:
- dimensions: min/max width, title-bar height, padding
- elevation and shadow in both themes (a floating panel over a dark grid must keep a visible boundary at ≥3:1 against the grid ground)
- focus order: the title bar is a focus stop with a label that says the arrow keys move it
- the collapsed look
- what happens on window resize (the mockup clamps)
- whether windows can be **resized** and **snapped/docked** to the right edge (**OPEN-W1**, see §7)

New icons:
- `b5-icon-window` (Windows menu)
- `b5-icon-window-collapse` and `b5-icon-window-expand` (or confirm reusing `chevron-collapse` / `chevron-expand`)
- `b5-icon-close` (the mockup uses "✕")
- `b5-icon-window-reset`

### 4.5 Grid: Plan, Compact, Fit
- **Plan:** fixtures sit on the layout grid exactly as today (MVR-derived or hand-placed cells).
- **Compact:** empty rows are removed, and each row is packed left to right in plan order. Real spacing is ignored and relative order is kept.
  - Applies to Console and Tests.
  - Display only: it never changes the stored layout.
  - Remembered per browser.
- **Fit:** zooms so the whole active layer, or every layer under All, fits the pane.
  - Mockup zoom range: 0.85–1.6 in Plan, 0.8–1.6 in Compact.
  - Tiles never shrink below 86 × 64 px. When that floor stops the layer fitting, the pane scrolls.
- **Rows** take the height of their tallest tile, so tiles with cells never overlap the next row.
- **Layer header** inside the pane: name, count, and the spacing mode in words ("compact — packed in plan order, real spacing ignored").
- **All layers** stacks each layer with its own header.
- **Spec:**
  - grid ground and grid-line roles (the mockup draws column lines only)
  - the layer header
  - the empty Unplaced state ("No unplaced fixtures — every fixture has a position from the MVR")

### 4.6 Live fixture tile (replaces today's tile anatomy; component-specs §3 is extended)

Anatomy in the mockup, top to bottom:
1. **Top row:** fixture glyph (20 px), **FID** (bold 15 px), **selection-order badge** when selected, and a **colour swatch** when colour is SET.
2. **Short type name** (12.5 px, secondary). This is the GDTF ShortName the app already stores, e.g. "Rayzor".
3. **Cells row** (pixel bars and multi-cell fixtures only): one segment per cell. Each segment shows its own intensity as a fill and its number (or its selection-order number when selected), and can be selected on its own.
4. **Intensity bar:** a fill to the current output level with the word `75%`. Tags sit at its right:
   - `SET`: has programmer values
   - `TEST`: a test is driving it (dashed)
   - `HL`: highlighted

States, each with a non-colour cue:

| State | Cue in the mockup |
|---|---|
| Selected | 3 px accent border + order badge |
| Cell selected | 2 px border on that segment + its order number |
| Has programmer values | `SET` tag |
| Test running | `TEST` tag + dashed outline outside the tile |
| Highlighted | `HL` tag + ring |
| Ghost / unprofiled / offline / unknown class | unchanged from component-specs §3 |

Specify:
- sizes: 86 × 64 minimum, 92 × 64 at zoom 1, +30 px for a cells row
- type, padding and truncation (FID never truncates; the name truncates with an ellipsis)
- the swatch, which is content and not a signal: brightness capped by `--b5-preview-max-brightness` in Dark
- the intensity fill and its contrast against the % word
- the tag mini-component
- the **cell segment touch target** (22 px in the mockup is fine for a mouse, but too small for coarse pointers; propose the coarse-pointer form)
- what the tile shows when "Names on tiles" or "Live values on tiles" is off

**Intensity source (for the spec text):**
- The bar should show the fixture's **composed output intensity**: the master times the parts, as the J1c/J1d model computes it.
- It is not just the programmer value.
- Production still has to confirm the engine exposes this per fixture and per cell. Not verified for this brief.
- When disarmed it shows what Arm would send, and the header already says "no output".
- Name this honestly in the tile's accessible label ("intensity 75 %, not on the wire" when disarmed).

### 4.7 Selection bar
- **Count pill:** `✓ 3 selected` (solid accent border). When nothing is selected: `Nothing selected` (dashed, regular weight).
- **Description:** a summary of what is selected, grouped by type with FID runs ("Wash 201–212 (12) · Pixel bar 401.2"). It truncates with an ellipsis, and its full text is in the title and the accessible name.
- **Primary actions**, always visible: Clear…, Highlight (toggle), Locate, Level 20 %…
- **More ▾:**
  - Lowlight
  - Fan…
  - Highlight previous / next
  - Store group…
  - Open Tests for the selection
- Spec the 1024 px composition.

### 4.8 Controls window body
- Same seven attribute tabs (icon, word, "n set"), laid out as a 7-column strip that fits a 440 px window.
- **Only the open tab's controls are shown.** Per-channel function details fold away under a "Channel details — modes, delay, raw DMX" disclosure. Today the Dimmer card alone runs about 1,300 px with the Dimmer-mode slot buttons and delay steps open.
- Dimmer tab: Master fader with 0 / 50 % / FULL and Fine, then the part faders.
- Spec the window at 440 px (and at its min/max width) for each tab. Reuse existing component specs §5–§14, re-flowed.

### 4.9 Tests tab (new nav item)
- **Nav:** a new item "Tests" after Console.
- **Layout:**
  - left: the same grid component with the same shared selection, plus a selection bar with its own Plan/Compact switch
  - right: the Tests panel (about 420–560 px)
- **Panel contents, top to bottom:**
  - header with "All tests off"
  - a sentence saying the selection is shared with Console
  - Scope: Selection / Stored group / Layer / All fixtures
  - Fade time
  - test tiles grouped Dimmer / Position / Colour / Beam / Focus / Shaper, each showing "n of n selected have this"
  - Isolate card
  - Sequences
- Test tiles keep component-specs §16 (four signals: word ON/OFF, icon, border, fill). The mockup uses a dashed info border + fill for ON.
- **Grid tiles under a running test** show `TEST` and the dashed outline (§4.6).
- Spec this at 1024 / 1440. Does the grid on Tests also need Lasso, Add and Select ▾? **OPEN-T1.**
- New icon: `b5-icon-nav-tests`, or confirm reusing `b5-icon-test`.

## 6. House rules that bind this work (unchanged)

- **Never colour alone.** Word first, then icon, then border weight or style, then hue. Desaturate the screen and check you can still tell states apart.
- **44 px targets on coarse pointers.** Use `--b5-size-touch-min`, never a literal.
- **Name the unknown.** Show "not reported" or "no position", never a plausible 0.
- **Wide content scrolls in its own box.** The page body never scrolls horizontally.
- **Universe labels** go through `UI.formatUser` / `UI.formatArtnet` / `UI.formatSacn`. This screen shows none; don't add any.
- **Pills always contain a word.**
- **Go and Stop are different shapes:** the ARM pill and the DISARM slab. The stop control is never hidden, disabled or moved.
- **Red is only for errors and destructive actions.** Amber means caution.
- **Dark venue:** no bright surfaces, colour previews capped, no animated attention-grabbers.
- **Sticky, never fixed,** for bars inside a screen. The floating windows are absolutely positioned inside the workspace, not `fixed` to the viewport.
- **Real `<button>` elements with real labels,** keyboard-reachable.
- **SVG delivery:** the owner's file transfer stamps SVG content with C2PA metadata. The repo has a guard test (J1d) that fails if the sprite carries it. Deliver clean SVG. Production integration adds the symbols to the existing sprite and keeps every existing symbol.

## 7. Open questions (answer in your package or ask)

- **OPEN-W1:**
  - Can floating windows be resized, and from which edge?
  - Should Controls snap/dock to the right edge so the grid's Fit leaves room for it, instead of floating over tiles?
  - Recommend one.
- **OPEN-W2:** Raw DMX and Tools: windows of their own, or stay where they are?
- **OPEN-T1:** On the Tests tab, does the grid get the full toolbar (Lasso, Add, Select ▾), or only click-to-select?
- **OPEN-K1:** Keyboard shortcuts to show/hide windows (e.g. `C` Controls, `O` Order, `G` Groups)? There are no global shortcuts in the app today, so propose them only if they can't fire while typing in a field.
- **OPEN-L1:** Does the tile's colour swatch show the programmer colour or the composed output colour (e.g. white under a dimmer test)? The mockup shows the programmer colour.
