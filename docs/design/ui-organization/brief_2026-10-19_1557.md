## 1. What you may decide, propose, or must leave alone

The owner wants you to make choices, not only draw what you're told.

**You decide.** Make the call, record it in `decisions.md` (§6) and show it in the scenes:
- **Layout within a screen:** section order, what sits side by side, column widths, sticky regions, spacing and density.
- **Moving controls:**
  - what stays visible
  - what goes under a menu, a "More", a disclosure or a row menu
  - what moves to an inspector
- **Help text:**
  - shortening it, or moving it into a "?" disclosure or tooltip
  - a per-browser "Show hints" switch if you want one
- **Copy:** labels, button wording, empty states. Keep the vocabulary in §5.
- **Tables:**
  - row height
  - sticky headers
  - which columns show by default
  - truncation
- **Visual hierarchy:** headings, section numbering, card vs no card, icon use.

**You propose with options.** Give each one 2–3 options and a recommendation; the owner chooses.
- **Navigation:** order and grouping of the main tabs, renaming a tab, merging or splitting screens.
- **Removing** any feature, or any control that has no other route to it.
- **Moving a control to another screen.**
- **Defaults:** any change to what is on or open by default.
- **Behaviour:** anything that changes what a control does, not only where it is.
- **Changes to W1's components:** any change to a W1 component's spec (header, toolbar, menu grammar, toggle ON state, dock and floating window, tile) needed to make it work on another screen.

**You leave alone (fixed).**
- **Output safety:**
  - ARM/DISARM, the lease, Blackout
  - the DISARM slab, its 60 px height (W1 spec §1) and its position
  - "Disarm always reachable, never hidden, never moved"
- **Apply-to-confirm:** the contract on screens that stage changes (Settings, Patch edits, node config) and its signed-off exceptions.
- **Destructive actions:**
  - every confirmation step stays
  - destructive controls never move next to everyday ones
- **The group-fader bar** on every screen; its behaviour is unchanged.
- **The house rules** in `console-workspace/ui-brief_2026-10-18_2249.md` §6 and W1's "Shared grammar": accessibility, colour, 44 px coarse targets, universe formatters, metadata-free SVG.

## 4. Recommendations (Claude's; accept, adapt or reject each with a reason in `decisions.md`)

### 4.1 App-wide
1. **W1's header on every screen, no repeated page headings.** The nav tab says where you are, so the per-screen `<h1>` becomes visually hidden; keep it for screen readers. That's about 60 px back on top of the header saving.
2. **One screen toolbar row,** using W1's toolbar and menu grammar:
   - primary actions on the left
   - secondary actions under **More ▾** or a named menu ("Import ▾", "Export ▾")
   - destructive actions only inside menus, after a separator, worded plainly
3. **Help on demand.**
   - Paragraphs become a "?" disclosure beside what they explain. An optional per-browser **Show hints** switch.
   - Text that never changes and is read once doesn't stay on screen.
4. **List + inspector** as the house pattern for anything with rows. The inspector is W1's dock (§2 table).
5. **Sticky table headers and denser rows,** keeping W1's coarse targets. A per-browser **Comfortable / Compact** row density pairs with the Console's Plan / Compact.
6. **Navigation** (propose; owner decides). My suggestion follows a day on site:
   - **Show:** Patch · Console · Tests · Rig Walk
   - **Network:** Nodes · Devices · Analyzer
   - **Settings** last
   - Shown as two labelled groups in the one nav row, or reordered with a divider.

### 4.2 Per screen
- **Patch:**
  - The table is the screen: header sticky, rows ≤ 48 px fine pointer (W1 targets on coarse).
  - Columns: Universe · Address (with footprint) · FID · Name · Type (short name, full in title) · RDM match.
  - Clicking a row opens the inspector, holding Edit, ↑/↓ and Delete (last, after a separator).
  - Checked rows show a bulk-action bar (W1 §7 pattern).
  - Toolbar: show picker · Add entry · **Import ▾** (MVR, GDTF) · **Adopt discovered ▾** (merge; replace patch…, marked destructive) · **Export ▾** (JSON, TXT) · New show… under More.
  - The universe-numbering paragraph becomes a "?" on the Universe column header.
- **Settings:**
  - A **section index** (sticky left column: Network, Universe numbering, sACN, Output, Capture & logging, Theme, Danger zone) beside one scrolling column, or one section at a time. Recommend one.
  - Per-field "?" help.
  - "Takes effect when Benny512 restarts" and "Takes effect at the next ARM" become short **word tags** on the field, because those are needed at a glance.
  - Danger zone stays last and separate.
- **Devices:**
  - Discovery controls + filters in one toolbar row.
  - "Clear discovered-device memory" moves into More ▾, behind its existing confirmation.
  - Gateway advertisements / RDM replies becomes a collapsible section or tab, closed by default once devices exist.
  - Device detail goes in the inspector.
- **Nodes:**
  - Keep list + detail.
  - Decide whether the detail becomes the shared inspector, for one pattern app-wide.
- **Analyzer:** the capture controls and packet list fit 1440 × 900; the list scrolls in its own box.
- **Rig Walk:** a focused, glove-friendly flow; counter and Next/Back stay in reach without scrolling.

## 7. Notes on W1 for the owner's review (Claude, from W1's 1440 evidence; fix in a W1 follow-up, not in W1b)
1. **Colour swatch reads as near-black in Dark.**
   - The tile swatch is drawn at `filter: brightness(var(--b5-preview-max-brightness))`, which is 0.12 in Dark (`workspace.css`), so a blue SET colour looks black.
   - That follows the dark-venue rule as Claude's W0 brief wrote it, but the rule was meant for large preview areas.
   - Propose a fix that keeps the venue dark and makes the colour identifiable: a minimum brightness floor for small swatches, an outline + colour name, or the name in the tile's accessible label and title.
2. **The "Beam/Gobo" tab label breaks mid-word** ("Beam/Go / bo") in the 440 px dock's tab strip. Break at the slash, shorten, or give that tab more width.
3. **Fit in Plan still scrolls sideways at 1440** because tiles have a minimum size (documented in W1 `verification.md`). Tell the owner up front that Compact is the no-scroll view, or add a hint when Fit can't fit.
